Mah Noor is a textile and embroidery designer with a deep-rooted passion for the intersection of craft and artistry. With over three years of professional experience, she has carved a distinct identity in Pakistan's fashion and textile industry — blending intricate hand-embroidery traditions with modern design principles.

Her journey began at Kohinoor, where she developed foundational expertise in textile techniques, colour theory, and fabric manipulation. This was further enriched through a prestigious programme at PNCA (Pakistan National Council of the Arts) — one of the country's most celebrated institutions for visual and applied arts.

Today, Mah Noor works at Kashni, where she leads seasonal embroidery motif development, creates production-ready layouts, and collaborates with creative and sampling teams to bring collections to life. Her design philosophy centres on the belief that textile art is a living language — one that carries cultural memory, personal expression, and contemporary vision simultaneously.

Whether developing intricate zardozi motifs for bridal wear, translating trend research into print collections, or illustrating fashion concepts for client presentations, Mah Noor brings precision, creativity, and a distinctly South Asian sensibility to every project.

She conceptualises motif libraries, creates production-ready embroidery layouts, and ensures design integrity from concept through to final garment.
